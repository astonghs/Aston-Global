About Us

Who we are

Aston Global Healthcare Services is an Ohio-based provider of compassionate, person-centered home and community based services.

Our Story

Serving Ohio communities since 2021

Aston Global Healthcare Services began in 2021, initially serving seniors and veterans through the Passport program.

In 2023, the company expanded into Ohio's developmental-disability service system and began providing DODD-related services. Since then, we've continued to expand our capabilities across a wide range of home and community based supports.

Our Mission

Empowering people. Supporting independence. Strengthening communities.

Our mission is to empower individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and other support needs to live independently, safely, meaningfully, and with dignity.

  • Person-centered services
  • Compassionate caregivers
  • Community inclusion
  • Advocacy
  • Independence-building
  • Transportation support
  • Health and safety
  • Meaningful relationships
  • Employment & community participation
  • Reliable communication
Our Vision

A community where everyone can live a meaningful life.

Aston Global envisions communities where individuals with disabilities and other support needs are respected, included, empowered, and given the resources necessary to pursue their goals.

Our Core Values

What we stand on

  • Compassion

    Kindness, patience, empathy, and understanding in every interaction.

  • Respect

    Every person's choices, rights, culture, privacy, and dignity.

  • Integrity

    We do what we say we will do and hold ourselves accountable.

  • Safety

    The health, safety, and well-being of every person we support comes first.

  • Independence

    We help people build skills and make choices, not do everything for them.

  • Inclusion

    Full participation in families, communities, workplaces, and social life.

  • Excellence

    Continuous improvement of services, staff development, and quality systems.
