Who We Serve

People we support

Our services are designed for individuals who may need assistance with daily living, community participation, or health and safety.

Who we work with

  • Intellectual disabilities
  • Developmental disabilities
  • Physical disabilities
  • Aging-related support needs
  • Seniors and veterans
  • Independent living support
  • Activities of daily living
  • Community participation
  • Transportation
  • Employment support
  • Health and safety
  • Respite and residential supports
Complex Support

Higher levels of assistance

We have experience supporting individuals who require higher levels of assistance. Capabilities are matched to each person's authorized plan and to staff who are trained and equipped for that specific support.

  • Medication support
  • Diabetes-related support
  • Mobility assistance
  • Wheelchair support
  • Hoyer lift transfers
  • G-tube / feeding-related support
  • Oxygen-related support
  • Seizure precautions
  • Respiratory equipment
  • Behavioral support
  • Delegated nursing support
  • Medically trained staff
  • Assistance with extensive ADLs
Person-Centered Care

The person comes first.

We do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Every person has different goals, strengths, preferences, abilities, communication styles, family relationships, cultural backgrounds, health needs, community interests, and dreams for the future.

Our staff work with the individual and their support team to understand what is important to the person and what is important for the person. Our goal is to provide the right level of support while encouraging the greatest possible level of independence.
