Nondiscrimination & Privacy

How we treat every person who comes to us, and how we handle the information you share through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Notice of Nondiscrimination

Aston Global Healthcare Services LLC provides services and employment opportunities without discrimination based on race, color, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, sex (including pregnancy, sexual orientation, and gender identity), religion, military status, or any other basis protected by law. We do not exclude people, deny them benefits, or treat them differently because of any of these characteristics.

Help with communication

We provide free aids and services to help people communicate with us effectively, including language interpretation, written information in other formats, and assistance for people with disabilities. To request any of these, call (419) 245-8113 or email admin@astonghs.com.

If you believe you have been treated unfairly

You have the right to raise a concern or file a complaint, and doing so will never affect the services you receive or lead to retaliation. You can:

  • Contact us directly at (419) 245-8113, admin@astonghs.com, or by mail at 5902 Southwyck Blvd, Suite 200-D, Toledo, OH 43614. We will review your concern promptly.
  • File a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, at ocrportal.hhs.gov or by phone at 1-800-368-1019 (TDD 1-800-537-7697).
  • File a complaint with the Ohio Civil Rights Commission at crc.ohio.gov.

Website Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information we collect through astonghs.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website. Information we receive while providing services to a person is handled under separate policies and the laws that apply to it, including HIPAA where it applies.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the Get Services form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, county, the kind of support you are looking for, and your message. If you call or email us, we receive the information you share with us that way.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, the services that host and deliver this site may automatically record things such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit.

Please do not include confidential medical or health information in the web form or in regular email. For anything sensitive, call us directly.

How we use it

We use the information you send to respond to your inquiry, learn what support you need, coordinate with your care team, and answer questions about working with us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a few outside services to run this website. Our contact form is processed by Formspree, which delivers your submission to us. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when you visit. We share information only as needed to operate the site and respond to you, or when the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No website or email is completely secure, which is why we ask you to call us for anything sensitive.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have received from you through this site, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it for legal reasons. Contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is intended for adults, including family members, guardians, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
