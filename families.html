For Families

You don't have to navigate everything alone.

Finding the right provider can feel overwhelming. We'll help you work through what's needed, what's authorized, and what comes next.

What we help you figure out

  • What support is needed
  • What services are authorized
  • What the individual wants
  • What the family is looking for
  • Which staffing arrangements may work best
  • How services can promote greater independence

Our goal is to make the transition into services as smooth as possible for the individual, the family, and the entire support team.

How It Works

Getting started, step by step

  • Reach out

    Call or email us. Tell us a little about your situation — no paperwork yet.

  • Talk through needs

    We discuss what support is needed, what's authorized, and what the person wants.

  • Coordinate with your team

    We connect with your SSA, county board, or case manager to line things up.

  • Start services

    We match the right staff, schedule a start date, and begin support.
