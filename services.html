Our Services

Services we provide

Every service below is built around the individual — their goals, their routines, their preferences. Support is delivered according to what is authorized in each person's service plan.

01
Homemaker Personal Care

Homemaker Personal Care (HPC)

Individualized HPC designed to help individuals remain safe, healthy, and independent at home and in the community. Support is delivered by trained Direct Support Professionals and shaped around each person's daily routine.

  • Personal care
  • Hygiene & dressing
  • Meal preparation
  • Household tasks
  • Medication-related support
  • Community participation
  • Shopping & appointments
  • Recreation
  • Skill development
  • Transportation
  • Safety & supervision
  • Independence-building
02
Residential Respite

Residential Respite

Temporary support and relief for individuals and the family members or caregivers who support them. Respite gives families a genuine break while the individual keeps their routines, their comfort, and their familiar level of care.

  • Safe temporary residential support
  • Personal care
  • Daily living assistance
  • Community activities
  • Recreation
  • Meal support
  • Health & safety monitoring
03
Remote Support

Remote Support Services

Technology-supported oversight that helps individuals maintain independence while still receiving appropriate monitoring and assistance. Our model includes coordination with the person's full support team, so remote support never means being on your own.

  • 24-hour monitoring
  • Scheduled wellness calls
  • Remote communication
  • Safety monitoring
  • Emergency response procedures
  • Technology-based support
  • Support team coordination

Our remote-support model can also include scheduled check-in calls throughout the day, tailored to each individual's monitoring needs.

04
Supported Living

Supported Living

Help living as independently as possible, with the right amount of assistance in the right places. The goal is always to build skills and confidence, not to do things for someone that they can learn to do themselves.

  • Independent living skills
  • Household management
  • Meal planning & shopping
  • Budgeting
  • Community participation
  • Transportation & appointments
  • Social activities
  • Health & safety
  • Relationship building
05
Shared Living

Shared Living

Individualized shared living support designed around the person's preferences, needs, goals, and desired lifestyle. Compatibility matters more than placement — we take the time to get the match right.

  • Choice
  • Compatibility
  • Independence
  • Safety
  • Community
06
Participant-Directed

Participant-Directed HPC

For individuals who choose participant-directed service options and want greater involvement in selecting and directing their own support. We work within that structure to make sure the person stays in the driver's seat.

07
Non-Medical Transportation

Non-Medical Transportation (NMT)

Transportation is often the difference between participating in your own life and staying home. We provide rides for authorized needs so people can get where they need to go.

  • Medical appointments
  • Employment
  • Day programs
  • Community activities
  • Shopping
  • Recreation
  • Personal errands
  • Other approved destinations
08
HPC Transportation

HPC Transportation

Transportation support tied to authorized HPC services and community participation, delivered by the same staff who already know the person and their routine.

Not sure which service fits?

Call us and we'll help you figure out what's authorized and what the next step looks like.
